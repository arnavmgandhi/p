About

I like the part of frontend work where layout, type and motion have to agree with each other, and the page still loads fast on a cheap phone.

Lately that means a club website with member and admin logins, a browser game, and offline-capable web apps. I care about readable code, accessible markup, and being straight about what I know well and what I’m still learning.
